The bundled syntax highlighters include the following MIT-licensed tree-sitter grammars:

- tree-sitter-bash
- tree-sitter-c
- tree-sitter-cpp
- tree-sitter-css
- tree-sitter-go
- tree-sitter-html
- tree-sitter-javascript
- tree-sitter-json
- tree-sitter-md
- tree-sitter-python
- tree-sitter-rust
- tree-sitter-toml-ng
- tree-sitter-typescript
- tree-sitter-yaml

The MIT license text is provided in tree-sitter-MIT.txt.
The tree-house and tree-house-bindings crates are MPL-2.0; their license is provided in tree-house-MPL-2.0.txt.
